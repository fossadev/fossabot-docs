Getting started is easy with Fossabot, simply head to [fossabot.com](https://fossabot.com) and click "Log In". You will be taken to an authorization screen from Twitch:

![Twitch authorization screen - Top of page](/img/docs/getting-started/twitch-auth-top.png)

Simply click "Authorize" at the bottom of the page to connect Fossabot with your Twitch account:

![Twitch authorization screen - Bottom of page](/img/docs/getting-started/twitch-auth-bottom.png)

Once you have done that, you'll be redirected back to Fossabot and it will automatically set you up with your own personal dashboard!

## Setting up your channel

The first time you log in, your dashboard opens a short setup guide. It walks you through:

- Joining Fossabot to your channel, so it can respond to commands and moderate your chat.
- Making Fossabot a moderator. On Twitch, YouTube and X, Fossabot can add itself with one click. On Kick, type `/mod Fossabot` in your chat.
- Linking any other Twitch, YouTube, Kick or X channels you stream on, so you can manage them all from one dashboard.
- Importing your settings from Nightbot or StreamElements, if you're switching from another bot.
- A quick tour of commands, blocked terms, spam filters and keywords.

You can skip any step, or close the guide at any time. Everything it covers is also on your dashboard afterwards. If you get stuck, [join the Discord](https://fossabot.com/discord) for help.
